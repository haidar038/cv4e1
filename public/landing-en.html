A send-ready CV, built on your own device.

cv4every1 is a free, open-source CV builder for Indonesian job seekers — fresh graduates first. No account, no server, no watermark. Once loaded, the core features keep working offline.

Open the app Source code

One source of data, two versions

Different openings call for different CVs. You enter your data once, then switch modes with one click — no retyping, no lost data.

ATS-oriented mode Creative mode
Single column, photo hidden automatically, restrained decoration — designed to be easy for tracking systems to read. Optional photo, two columns, color accents — for startups, agencies, or direct applications to HR.

What we do not promise: no guaranteed ATS pass, no CV score. Tracking systems differ and are closed; what we give you are verifiable properties — extractable text, standard headings, predictable reading order.

How to use it

  1. Fill in the guided form (GPA, education status, campus organizations).
  2. Pick the ATS or Creative mode and check the preview.
  3. Export a PDF to send, export JSON for backup.

Your data stays yours

  • Your CV lives in this device's browser storage — not on our servers (there are none).
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party scripts.
  • The optional AI features use your own key and never change data without approval.
  • An honest note: clearing browser data or private mode can delete drafts — use Export as backup, and full wipe is always available.

Questions

I have no work experience.
